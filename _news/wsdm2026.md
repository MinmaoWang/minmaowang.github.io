Excited to announce that our paper **"TemporalExpertNet: Cross-Temporal Knowledge Reuse for Promotion-Aware CVR Prediction"** has been accepted by **WSDM 2026** (The 19th ACM International Conference on Web Search and Data Mining) as an **Oral Presentation**, and was awarded **Best Paper Runner-up**!

WSDM is a top-tier conference (CCF-B) in the field of web search, data mining, and information retrieval. This work proposes a novel framework for cross-temporal knowledge reuse in promotion-aware conversion rate prediction, addressing key challenges in e-commerce recommendation systems.
