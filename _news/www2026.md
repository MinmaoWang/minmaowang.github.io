Excited to announce that our paper **"Hierarchical Semantic RL: Tackling the Problem of Dynamic Action Space for RL-based Recommendations"** has been accepted by **WWW 2026** (The International World Wide Web Conference) as an **Oral Presentation**!

WWW is a top-tier conference (CCF-A) in the web and information retrieval community. This work introduces a hierarchical semantic reinforcement learning framework to effectively address the dynamic action space problem in RL-based recommendation systems.
